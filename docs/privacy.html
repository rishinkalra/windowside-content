Windowside Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Windowside shows you what's outside your plane window. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your location stays on your phone, there's no account, no ads and no tracking.

Your location

Windowside uses your phone's GPS to show where the plane is and what you can see. Your location is used on your phone only. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and we don't keep a history of it anywhere but your phone (the app may remember the path of a past flight on your phone, to draw the same route better next time).

What the app downloads

To work offline, Windowside downloads its map and place information from GitHub. To show stories and photos of places along your route, it fetches articles and images from Wikipedia, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons. These requests name the place, not you or your location. Like any website, these services can see your device's IP address when it connects, under their own privacy policies.

No account, no ads, no tracking

Windowside has no sign-in, shows no ads, and contains no analytics or tracking software. We don't sell or share personal information.

Bug reports in test versions

If you're a tester using a test version of Windowside and you choose to send a bug report, the report contains what you add (a screenshot or a short screen recording of the app, and your note, typed or dictated) plus technical details to help fix the problem: the app version, your device model and Android/iOS version, recent app log lines, and the flight you've selected (departure and arrival airports, never your position). Dictation is turned into text on your phone; your voice isn't recorded or sent. Reports are sent over an encrypted connection to a server we run, used only to fix bugs, and not shared with anyone. Ask us and we'll delete your reports. The public version of the app has no bug-report feature and sends nothing of this kind.

Children

Windowside is not directed at children under 13.

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
