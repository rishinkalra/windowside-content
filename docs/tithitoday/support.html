A festival date is different from my family calendar

Hindu festivals follow the Moon and the time of sunrise where you are, so the same festival can fall on different days in different cities. Tithi Today works the date out for the city you've chosen. Tap the festival to see exactly why it falls on that day, with the date in India next to it. If your city is far from India, the two can differ by a day. If you still think a date is wrong, write to us with the festival, the city and the year.

Purnimant or Amavasyant?

North India usually ends each lunar month at Purnima (purnimant); Gujarat, Maharashtra and the South end it at Amavasya (amavasyant). Festival dates stay the same either way; only the month name in the dark half changes. Choose yours in Settings, under Lunar month.

Smarta or Vaishnava Ekadashi?

Smarta keeps Ekadashi on the day it is current at sunrise. Vaishnava (including ISKCON) moves the fast to the next day when Dashami is still running 96 minutes before sunrise. Choose yours in Settings, under Ekadashi.

I'm not getting reminders

Turn on reminders in My dates, then allow notifications for Tithi Today in your phone's Settings. Check that a Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them. Reminders come at 7:00 am on the day and the day before.

Changing my city, language or look

Tap the city name at the top of Today or Calendar to choose another, or "Use my location". Language, Light or Dark, and your tradition are in Settings: the gear on the My dates tab.

Adding the widget

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Tithi Today. Open the app once after changing your city so the widget picks it up.
