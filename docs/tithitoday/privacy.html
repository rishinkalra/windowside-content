Tithi Today Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Tithi Today is a Hindu calendar that works out the tithi, festivals and auspicious times for where you are. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything is worked out on your phone, and nothing about you leaves it. There's no account, no ads and no tracking.

Your location

Sunrise, the tithi and festival dates depend on where you are, so Tithi Today asks for a city. You can pick one from the list, or tap "Use my location" to let the app find the nearest city. All the calculations run on your phone, and your location and chosen city are stored only on your phone. The app does not send your location anywhere.

Birthdays and other dates you save

Dates you save in My dates, and their names, are stored only on your phone. Reminders for them are scheduled by your phone; nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

Calendar and sharing

If you add a festival to your calendar, the app asks iOS for permission and adds that one event. It does not read your calendar. If you share a greeting card, it goes only where you choose to send it.

No account, no ads, no tracking

Tithi Today has no sign-in, shows no ads, and contains no analytics or tracking software. It makes no network requests of its own. We don't collect, sell or share personal information.

Moon pictures

The pictures of the Moon come from NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio and are built into the app.

Deleting your data

Everything the app keeps is on your phone: your city, language, tradition, saved dates and settings. Deleting the app removes all of it. We hold no data about you to delete.

Children

Tithi Today is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
