Windowside Support

Help with Windowside, the app that shows what's outside your plane window.

Contact us

Email rishin.kalra@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model, and if it's about a flight, the route and roughly when. We usually reply within two days.

Does it work in airplane mode?

Yes. GPS doesn't need a phone signal or Wi-Fi, so it keeps working in airplane mode. Open Windowside and pick your flight before you board, while you still have internet, so the map and stories for your route are saved on your phone.

The plane isn't moving on the map

Your phone needs to see the sky to find the GPS. Hold it near the window for a minute or two; in the middle of the cabin, or with the window shade down, it can take longer or not work at all. Make sure Location is allowed for Windowside in your phone's settings, with Precise Location on. If the signal drops mid-flight, Windowside estimates where you are from your route and take-off time, and snaps back as soon as the GPS returns.

I don't get the "look out of your window" alerts

Allow notifications for Windowside, and allow location "While Using the App" or "Always", so it can keep following the flight with the screen locked. Check that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them.

Can I try it without flying?

Yes. On the home screen pick any route, then tap "Watch a 3-minute demo of this flight" and choose a seat. The demo plays the flight fast so you can see how it works.

Which side should I sit on?

Either is fine. Tell Windowside whether you're at the left window, the right window or on the aisle, and it points you to the sights you can see from there. On the aisle, it tells you which side to lean towards.

Privacy

Your location stays on your phone. There's no account, no ads and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
